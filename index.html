Welcome message

Welcome Back to
Our Alma Mater

Years may have passed, but the memories we created at Telangana Tribal Welfare Residential Upgraded Junior College (TGTWURJC), Jinnaram remain close to our hearts.

From morning assemblies and classrooms to hostel life, sports, cultural events, examinations, and lifelong friendships, this campus shaped who we are today. The Grand Reunion 2026 is our opportunity to reconnect with classmates, honor our beloved teachers, revisit cherished memories, and create new ones together.

Once a TGTWURJC student,
always a part of the TGTWURJC family.

Same ground.
Same pride.
New chapters.

The ties that time could not undo.

We don’t only look back. We return to give something forward.

Telangana Tribal Welfare Residential Upgraded Junior College has played an important role in nurturing generations of students through quality education, discipline, values, and character.

The institution has provided opportunities for academic excellence while fostering leadership, teamwork, respect, and lifelong friendships. For every student from the 2007–2013 batches, this campus represents unforgettable memories that continue to inspire us throughout life.

Today, we reunite not just as former classmates, but as one family connected forever by our alma mater.

School Administration Guidelines

A few shared commitments will help us celebrate with respect for the school, its students, and every guest.

Restricted Access

Stay within designated event areas unless accompanied by school representatives.

Respect School Property

Classrooms, labs, library, and sports facilities need prior permission before use.

Photography & Videography

Capture memories responsibly and follow the school’s privacy policies.

Appropriate Conduct

Maintain discipline, respectful language, and the dignity of the institution.

No Political or Commercial Activities

Non-political, non-religious, non-commercial event. No promotions or advertising.

No Public Criticism

Share constructive suggestions privately after the event — not publicly on campus.

Alcohol-Free Event

No alcohol on campus. Anyone under the influence will not be permitted to attend.

Follow Safety Instructions

Follow directions from school authorities and volunteers, including emergency procedures.

Keep the Campus Clean

Use dustbins and dispose of waste responsibly throughout the event.

Parking & Traffic

Park only in designated areas and follow volunteer directions.

Food & Refreshments

Eat only in designated areas and help keep those spaces clean.

Let’s honour this campus the way it once honoured us with care, pride, and respect.
