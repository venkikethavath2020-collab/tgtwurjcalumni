About the association

Who we are

The TGTWURJC Alumni Association

We bring together former students of Telangana Tribal Welfare Upgraded Residential Junior College, Jinnaram — across every batch and year — to stay connected, support one another, and give back to the campus that shaped us.

What started as shared memories is growing into a lasting community — connecting alumni, teachers, and students while creating opportunities to support the next generation.

16 August 2026

Grand Reunion 2026
was a grand success.

Alumni from the 2007–2013 batches returned to TGTWURJC, Jinnaram. We laughed, remembered, thanked our teachers, and took the first steps toward a lasting alumni association — under the theme “Giving Back to Our School.”

To everyone who travelled home, volunteered, contributed, and shared the day: thank you. The bonds that brought us back will carry us forward — this day is the beginning of something larger.

Our purpose

Why We Exist

We were once students here. Today, we are professionals, entrepreneurs, educators, and members of society.

Our institution gave us the foundation to build our lives and careers. Now, we believe it is our responsibility to give back.

Through the Alumni Association, we want to connect former students with the students of today — sharing knowledge, experience, opportunities and guidance to help them build a better future.

What we received from our institution,
let’s pass it on to the next generation.
